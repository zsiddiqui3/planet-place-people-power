The land cover of Lyari Town

The question

At Lyari Town in Karachi, land cover and flooding go together because of extreme urbanization over historical drainage pathways, and I could check it with historical drainage maps, development patterns, and modern flood reports.

The data

The layer used is Land Cover from 2026, made by Airbus, CNES/Airbus, Landsat/Copernicus, and Maxar Technologies. What's missing is a map of drainage and sewage systems as well as a neighborhood boundary. The layer is standing in for a more detailed land cover map.

What I found

This map supports the idea that this neighborhood no longer has water diffusion methods for when the river overflows. The density of the urban space makes it difficult for both floodwater and rainwater to properly drain. The proximity of the neighborhood with the river makes this a very pressing issue for residents.

What this does not show

This image can not prove if there is an established drainage system that can handle the demand. We must assume that without the aid of natural drainage, current systems are overwhelmed by the water it needs to control. With a zoomed in view we can see the density of the neighborhood, but not how it compares to the density of the city or how many green spaces there are in other areas. A one step zoom out could show us if other neighborhoods do have natural drainage methods.
