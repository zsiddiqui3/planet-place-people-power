Pakistan's Exports and Origins

The question

Do Pakistan's natural resources create it's largest export, to which places are exports distributed around the world, and at what scale do Pakistan's top exports rely on the ground?

The data

Data was collected from 2024 and used from the the Atlas of Economic Complexity from The Growth Lab at Harvard University and joined with the boundaries of 258 countries at 1:110m, from the Natural Earth database, Admin 0 - Countries, Version 5.1.1. The Natural Earth license as stated by them: "All versions of Natural Earth raster + vector map dat found on this website are in the public domain." The Atlas used was tracked exports in USD, used version 24.4.1. It's classified as Harmonized System 1992 (HS92). Its license statement is: Reuse and adaptation are encouraged, provided users cite the Atlas and its underlying data sources appropriately.
