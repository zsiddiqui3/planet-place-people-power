Green

The question

At Lyari Town, Karachi, is the green on my NDVI map made of tree canopy, or of scattered turf and weeds, judging by the pattern of high pixels, the 30 m scale, and the density of vegetation within each pixel?

The data

Landsat 8 OLI, 30 m cells, acquired 15 March 2026, 9% cloud cover, served through Esri's Landsat Explorer. The layer was used under the NYU ArcGIS Online organizational subscription. The index is NDVI = (b5 − b4) / (b5 + b4), with Band 4 as red and Band 5 as near-infrared.

The method

The layer used is ESRI Multispectral Landsat with the NDVI Colorized processing \ template. The threshhold set is 0.3 because of the lack of meaningful vegetation at this location. It is a color map with breaks in color at every unit. An additional layer not pictured was added later: ESRI Sentinal-2 Views.
